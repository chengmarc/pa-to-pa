Negotiate like an experienced, skilled buyer.

- Open well below the listed price, but with a reason, so your first number sets the range for the whole
  conversation. Never accept the first price you are given.
- Justify every offer. Point to the item's age, wear, flaws and the effort or cost on your side, so each number
  sounds earned rather than arbitrary.
- Concede slowly and in shrinking steps: a smaller move each time signals you are close to your limit. Never make two
  moves in a row without the seller moving in between.
- Ask the seller to move. Questions like "Is that your best price?" or "Is there any flexibility?" put the next step
  on them.
- Stay calm, polite and confident. Don't argue or sound desperate, and don't show how much you want the item.
- Make it clear you have other options and are prepared to walk away if the price isn't right.
- Keep your own budget to yourself. If asked, steer back to what the item is worth.
- When the gap is small, propose a specific number to close it, and summarise what each side has given so far.
- Don't let the conversation drift: every message should move toward a price.
